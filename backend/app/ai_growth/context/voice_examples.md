# Voice Examples

Few-shot anchors. Read by two things:

- **The generate node** — as tone anchors for new drafts.
- **The LLM judge** — as the reference standard for `human_feel` and
  `language_fit`.

---

## READ THIS FIRST — every fact below is false

Every tournament name, date, prize pool, and platform statistic in this file is
from a past run. **PEC 2026, MIDI Esports Masters, RM3,000, 8–9 Ogos, "8
tournament", "RM14k+" — none of these are current. Treat all of them as expired.**

They are here because **the specificity is the voice**. A vague post is a dead
post. Copy the shape of the specificity, never the content of it.

Every factual claim in a new post comes from the tournament data in the prompt,
or from `trends.md`. Nowhere else. Never from this file.

## Threads

### utility — tournament spotlight

> PEC 2026 kat Pahang dah masuk listing. 8–9 Ogos, MLBB, prize pool RM3,000.
> Tarikh, slot, deadline, link register — semua dalam satu page. Tak payah dah
> korek story repost. Link kat bio.

Facts arrive as a fragment. The value proposition is one clause, not a
paragraph. No hashtags, no exclamation marks.

### utility — deadline nudge

> Friendly reminder: deadline register MIDI Esports Masters minggu ni. Kalau
> team korang belum settle, ni la masa dia. Details penuh ada kat site.

*"Friendly reminder"* is English and stays English — a borrowed-whole idiom, not
imported marketing. *"ni la masa dia"* is an offer. Compare against anything
implying regret.

### utility — organiser-facing

> Organiser tournament: listing kat Esportorium free. Paste je poster, sistem
> tolong draft-kan, kitorang review sebelum naik. Tak ambik cut, tak ambik fee —
> kitorang cuma nak semua tournament senang dijumpai.

Second audience, same voice. No corporate register. Signposted in the first two
words so the reader knows immediately it isn't for them.

### relatable — platform value

> Cara lama cari tournament: scroll IG, zoom poster 400% nak baca deadline,
> rupanya dah tutup. Kitorang buat Esportorium sebab kitorang pun penat dengan
> benda ni. Semua tournament MLBB Malaysia, satu tempat, boleh filter ikut
> negeri.

The middle sentence is load-bearing. Without *"kitorang pun penat"* this is an
ad. With it, it's a confession that happens to be an ad. Problem → solution →
CTA with no confession beat is the failure mode.

### relatable — the poster

> Poster dengan enam jenis font dan deadline kecik kat corner bawah? Takpe,
> hantar je. Sistem kitorang boleh baca, dan kitorang susunkan jadi listing yang
> kemas. Memang itu kerja kitorang.

Affectionate, not teasing. *"enam jenis font"* is funny because it's specific.
*"Takpe"* does the softening. The joke's target is the work, never the organiser.

### relatable — community question

> Soalan untuk korang: prefer tournament online yang boleh join dari mana-mana,
> atau offline dengan crowd kat dewan? Kitorang nak tahu jenis mana patut
> di-highlight lagi kerap. Komen bawah.

A real question with a reason attached. Not engagement bait.

### relatable — team life

> Roster lengkap, semua confirm boleh main, form dah isi... pastu ada sorang
> cakap "eh weekend tu aku ada kenduri". Klasik. Takpe, tournament lain sentiasa
> ada — check je kat site.

**The reference post for laughing *with*.** The team did everything right —
*"form dah isi"* — and the failure is comic and external. *"kenduri"* is the
highest-signal word in the file; it can't be reached by translation.

Contrast with the failure mode: *"dah 3 bulan cakap nak join, tak pernah sekali
pun isi form, padahal ada tournament kecik"*. Same joke, aimed at the reader.
That's laughing *at*, and it's out.

### build_in_public — BTS / curation

> BTS sikit: setiap listing kitorang check manual — link register hidup ke tak,
> tarikh betul ke tak, organiser boleh dihubungi ke tak. Ambik masa sikit, tapi
> sebab tu apa yang naik memang confirmed hidup.

Three concrete checks, not "we care about quality". The claim is verifiable.

### build_in_public — milestone

> Update kecik: 8 tournament dah listed, RM14k+ total prize pool atas site.
> Platform ni baru lagi, tapi semua listing tu reviewed dan confirmed hidup.
> Slow slow, kita sampai.

**The anchor for inclusive *kita*.** The shift from *kitorang* to *kita* in the
last three words is what makes it land — brand and scene on the same side.

**Do not copy the counting.** `brand.md` forbids platform statistics in
generated posts, and this pipeline has no live source for them. Copy the posture
— humble, honest about being early — and the pronoun shift. The numbers are
expired and unreproducible.

### scene_trend — patch riff

> Patch baru buang [hero] dari meta, dan tiba-tiba semua orang kena belajar
> draft balik. Tournament weekend ni akan jadi menarik. Sapa main minggu ni,
> good luck — check jadual kat site.

*Contoh — isi sebenar ikut `trends.md`.* The bracketed slot rotates every run;
the structure and voice don't. The patch claim is a factual claim about the real
world and must exist in `trends.md`. *"good luck"* is a borrowed idiom and stays
English.

## TikTok

Structure is fixed: hook, at most 3 scenes, CTA. The voice is identical to
Threads — shorter lines, same register.

### relatable — platform value

- **Hook (0–2s):** "Korang tahu tak berapa banyak tournament MLBB tengah jalan
  kat Malaysia sekarang?" *(screen: scroll listing page)*
- **Scene 1:** Cara lama — screenshot poster, zoom sampai pecah, cari deadline.
  *(screen recording, zoom blur)*
- **Scene 2:** "Kitorang buat Esportorium sebab kitorang pun penat dengan benda
  ni. Buka site, filter negeri, semua satu page." *(clean screen recording)*
- **CTA:** "Free je. Link kat bio."

*"zoom sampai pecah"* is more idiomatic than "zoom 400%" — same joke, better
Malay. Scene 2 keeps the confession beat.

### utility — deadline PSA

- **Hook:** "Reminder baik hati untuk semua team." *(text on screen, deadpan)*
- **Scene 1:** Ni deadline yang tutup minggu ni. *(show listings, dates
  highlighted)*
- **Scene 2:** "Slot, fee, link organiser — semua dah kat satu tempat."
- **CTA:** "Kalau team korang belum settle, ni la masa dia. Link kat bio."

The CTA is lifted from the deadline nudge post — an offer to a team assumed to
be busy. Not a needle aimed at a team assumed to be procrastinating.

### relatable — ranked to tournament

- **Hook:** "Main ranked tiap malam dengan team yang sama?"
- **Scene 1:** "Ranked memang syok. Tapi bracket, crowd, nama team korang atas
  screen — tu lain macam."
- **Scene 2:** "Tournament kecik pun ada — ada yang online je, ada yang dekat.
  Semua dalam listing."
- **CTA:** "Bila korang ready, semua ada kat site. Link kat bio."

Grants that what they're already doing is good, then offers more. *"Bila korang
ready"* leaves the door open rather than pushing them through it. Same posture as
the team-life post.
